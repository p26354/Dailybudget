Privacy and security

This website has no server and no database. Your budget never leaves your device unless you download a backup yourself.

Stays on your device

Everything you enter is saved in your own browser's storage. Nobody else — including the site owner — can see it.

No accounts, no bank credentials

There's nothing to sign up for, and DailyBudget never asks for bank passwords, card numbers or account numbers.

No tracking or AI

No analytics, advertising trackers or AI services. Your transactions are not sent anywhere.

Back up and move anytime

Download a backup file, restore it on another phone or computer, export CSV, or erase everything with one click.
